# Intent Statement: Employee Management Web Application

## Problem Statement

The organization requires a unified web portal to centralize fragmented employee records, team assignments, and task tracking workflows. Currently, operational and HR data is scattered across ad-hoc tools, causing tracking delays, manual overhead, and a lack of real-time visibility into workforce distribution and project progress [desc] [Q1].

## Target Customer

The primary users are internal HR administrators and company leadership who need comprehensive, real-time visibility into employee profiles (including photo, position, age), cross-functional team structures, and company-wide operational statistics [desc] [Q2].

## Success Metrics

- **100% Digital Profile & Workflow Coverage**: Complete digitization of all employee profiles (name, age, position, profile picture), team rosters, and task state tracking (Todo, Pending, Completed) with zero record loss [desc] [Q3].
- **Real-Time Operational Visibility**: Instantaneous dashboard rendering of company data, team capacities, and task progression metrics [desc] [Q3].
- **Administrative Efficiency**: Elimination of manual spreadsheet-based tracking and reduced onboarding and team task coordination overhead [Q1] [Q3].

## Initiative Trigger

This initiative is driven by a modernization push to replace disparate, ad-hoc tracking spreadsheets and manual coordination tools with a robust, integrated company platform [Q4].

## Initial Scope Signal

- **Workflow-Selected Scope**: `enterprise` (comprehensive end-to-end lifecycle across ideation, inception, construction, and operation) [scope].
- **User-Confirmed Product Boundary**: Confirmed enterprise scope for building the full employee management web application, including employee profiles, multi-team assignments, 3-stage task management, and an executive data dashboard [desc] [Q8].
