# Stakeholder Map: Employee Management Web Application

## Key Stakeholders & Interests

| Stakeholder Role | Primary Interest & Focus | Authority Level | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Product Owner / Project Sponsor** | Defining overall product direction, approving scope boundaries, evaluating business alignment and stage progression | Primary Decision-Maker | [Q6] |
| **HR & People Operations** | Maintaining accurate employee profiles, team assignments, directory data integrity, and internal record consistency | Key Stakeholder / Domain Expert | [Q5] |
| **Department & Team Leads** | Allocating team tasks, managing workflows across Todo/Pending/Completed states, and monitoring team capacity | Key Stakeholder / Operational Lead | [Q5] |
| **Executive Leadership** | High-level organizational oversight, viewing company-wide statistics via dashboard analytics, tracking strategic velocity | Influencer / Executive Consumer | [Q5] |

## Decision-Makers vs. Influencers

- **Primary Decision-Maker**: Product Owner / Project Sponsor holds final sign-off on scope priorities, trade-offs, and deliverable approvals [Q6].
- **Key Influencers**: HR Administrators and Team Leads provide operational feedback on workflow usability and team organization dynamics [Q2] [Q5].

## Communication Requirements & Cadence

| Stakeholder Group | Mechanism / Cadence | Purpose | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Product Owner / Project Sponsor** | Standard milestone demonstrations and stage approval reviews | Reviewing lifecycle stage deliverables and approving progression gates | [Q7] |
| **HR & Management** | Structured feature reviews and preview walkthroughs | Validating profile fields, team assignment models, and dashboard metrics | [Q2] [Q7] |
